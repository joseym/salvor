About this canvas

What this is, and how the canvas works.

Builder authors a graph; Run projects a real run's route onto it, folded from GET /v1/runs/<id>/graph — the road actually taken, never a designer's happy path.

Forking a node replays the origin's recorded prefix and re-executes from there. Every write in the re-walked segment runs a second time; the hazard review makes you acknowledge each, and your acknowledgement is written permanently into the new run's log.
